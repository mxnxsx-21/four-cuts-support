Four Cuts Photo Booth

Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

The short version

Four Cuts Photo Booth collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no internet connection of any kind. Your photos stay on your device.

What the app accesses, and why

Camera

Used to take the four photos that make up a strip. The camera feed is shown on screen and captured to your strip. It is never transmitted anywhere.

Photo library

Used in two places, both at your request: choosing existing photos to build a strip from, and saving a finished strip. The app does not otherwise browse or read your library.

Motion sensors

Used to tell which way you are holding the device, so photos come out the right way up. These readings are used as the photo is taken, and are not stored or sent anywhere.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose to share a strip yourself. When you tap share, your device's own share sheet hands the image to whichever app you pick. From that point the receiving app's privacy policy applies, not ours.

Data we hold

None. There are no servers, no accounts and no third-party services. Strips and settings are stored only on your device, and deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects none from children either.
